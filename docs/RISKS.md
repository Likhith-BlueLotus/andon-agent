**#27 (2026-10-17) Client disconnect does not cancel a live run on Cloud Run.** Evidence: a live S005 stream cut by the client at 15 s ran 128 s server-side and completed through the action drafter (Cloud Logging `andon.llm` lines, run e-38da4b26). Cancellation works locally and in tests (api/sse.py `pump.cancel()`), but Cloud Run's front end keeps reading the response, so the app never sees the disconnect. Impact: a closed tab still costs one run (~$0.15). Bounded by per-IP live limits, 30 live runs/day and the $8/day kill switch. Mitigation after submission: client heartbeat/ack with a server-side idle cutoff. Owner: A.

**#28 (2026-10-17) A connection dropped mid-response failed the whole run.** Evidence: post-hoc eval `posthoc_v4`, 10 of 87 runs ended `internal` on `httpx.ReadError('')`, always two concurrent runs at the same moment (local uplink). google-genai retries timeouts and connect errors, not `ReadError` / `RemoteProtocolError`. Fix: `PinnedGemini` retries those up to 2 times with backoff, only before any chunk is yielded, so no output is duplicated (tests in `tests/test_adk_budget.py`). Residual: a drop after streaming has started still fails the run, and the UI's retry button covers it. Owner: A.
